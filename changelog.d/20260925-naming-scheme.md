### Added

- **Hostwarden can detect, propose and remember a fleet naming
  scheme.** After a few hosts are onboarded it recognises a shared
  hostname pattern and asks you to confirm it, or proposes a
  best-practice scheme on request. New hosts follow the confirmed
  scheme right away; existing ones are never renamed on their own —
  the fleet audit's new Naming row just flags a name that no longer
  fits.
